About Gen-Ardent

Professional Immigration Guidance with a Global Outlook

Gen-Ardent Immigration Inc Sri Lanka supports clients who want to migrate, study, work, visit and settle in Canada and other leading destinations.

Company introduction

Canada-Focused, Globally Connected

Gen-Ardent presents a professional consultancy experience for Sri Lankan and international clients. The web application highlights Canada as the priority country while also covering Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, the United States and Europe.

  • Head Office: Kollupitiya, Sri Lanka
  • Regional Office: Atlantic Provinces, Canada
  • Sri Lankan Branch: Galle, Sri Lanka

Client-Centered Process

Every pathway begins with profile understanding, eligibility review and transparent next steps.
