# Storyboard

1. A visitor opens the Home page and sees Gen-Ardent branding, the tagline and immigration promotions.
2. The visitor searches for `Canada visa` and opens the Canada Immigration page from the search result.
3. The visitor reads service cards and expands FAQ items to understand the process.
4. The visitor selects an interest in the recommendation box and receives a suggested pathway.
5. The visitor submits an inquiry or moves to Consultation Booking.
6. The visitor completes the consultation form and sees a success message.
7. The visitor tests chatbot prompts such as `hi`, `scholarship`, `legal service` and `office location`.
8. A job applicant opens Career, selects a role, uploads a CV and submits the application form.
