# Wireframe Notes

## Shared Layout

Header with logo, navigation, search, theme toggle and mobile menu. Footer with newsletter, social links, service links and official external links.

## Home

Hero image, promotional cards, service category grid, testimonial cards, success story CTA and newsletter band.

## Service Pages

Page hero, service card grid, promotional CTA, FAQ accordion, recommendation block where relevant and inquiry form.

## Consultation / Career / Contact

Form-first layouts with supporting cards beside or below the form. All fields use labels, error messages and success feedback.

## Mobile Layout

Navigation collapses into a menu button. Cards stack into one column. Search can be opened from the icon button.
